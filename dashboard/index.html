Insider buying

Each weekday before the European open, a scheduled Claude session reads the day's top-ranked stories from Google News, the BBC, the ECB, the European Commission, the US Federal Register and GDELT. It writes down the market-relevant events with their likely winners and losers, and a scoring model turns them into picks across large US and European stocks and sector, theme and country ETFs.

A research tool for personal use, not investment advice. It never places trades.
